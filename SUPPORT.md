# Support

Personal Finance is a free project maintained by one person in their own time; help is best effort.

- **Install or first-run problems** — check [docs/desktop-app.md](docs/desktop-app.md) (desktop app),
  [docs/windows.md](docs/windows.md#troubleshooting) (Windows with Docker), [docs/desktop.md](docs/desktop.md)
  (macOS or Linux with Docker) or [docs/deployment.md](docs/deployment.md) (server).
- **Broker connections** — [docs/broker-integrations.md](docs/broker-integrations.md) and the step-by-step guide in the
  app (*Connections*).
- **Bugs and feature ideas** — [open an issue](https://github.com/joaoguimaraespro/personal-finance/issues/new/choose).
- **Security problems** — privately, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Logs that help: `app.log` in the desktop app's data folder, `logs/launcher.log` on the Docker installs for a
computer, `docker compose logs api` on a server. Remove amounts, names, account numbers and keys before sharing them.
